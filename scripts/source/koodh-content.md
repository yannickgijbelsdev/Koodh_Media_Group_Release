FAQ

## Good to *know.*

The questions we hear most about radio imaging, jingles, photography and how we deliver.

What kind of audio branding do you make?

Jingles, promos, sweepers and show elements. We like to sit down together a few times during the process, so we really get to know your company and the sound fits your brand.

What kind of photography do you offer?

Live events, festivals, concerts, companies and portfolios. We capture the energy of the moment and deliver edited photos fast, so you can share them while the buzz is still there.

How far do you travel for a shoot?

We’re based in Budel-Schoot, but we drive all across Belgium and the Netherlands. There’s no fixed radius: wherever it happens, we come to you.

Do you have your own photo studio?

No. We shoot on location: at your event, your venue or a spot that fits the story.

What is included in the price?

For a shoot, the price covers the travel to and from the location, the gear we bring, the shoot itself and all the editing afterwards, so you end up with a finished result.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope and, for photography, on where the location is, since travel is part of the price. Every job is different, so the easiest way is to ask us for a quote.
